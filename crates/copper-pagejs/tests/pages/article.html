The quiet browser

Copper is a browser that stays out of the way. It has one field, the tabs, and the page, and almost nothing else on the screen at any one time. The page is the point, and the browser is the frame around it, kept as thin as it can be.

Reading mode takes the article and leaves everything that was arranged around it: the navigation, the rails of related links, the comments and the buttons.

The heuristic is old and it holds up: the piece of the page carrying the most prose, punished for every link inside it, is the article.
